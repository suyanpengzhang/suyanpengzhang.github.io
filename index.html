Postdoctoral Scholar, Department of Industrial Engineering and Management Sciences, Northwestern University

I am a postdoctoral scholar at Northwestern University, working with Professor Sanjay Mehrotra. Before this, I earned my Ph.D. in Industrial and Systems Engineering from the University of Southern California in 2024, advised by Professor Sze-Chuan Suen. I also hold an M.S. in Industrial and Operations Engineering from the University of Michigan (2019) and a B.S. in Mathematics from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (2017).

My research interests include healthcare analytics, dynamic programming, and optimization methods. I am also interested in interdisciplinary areas that combine optimization, statistics, probability, and artificial intelligence. Topics I have been working on recently:

  • Markov decision processes with applications in healthcare
  • Approximate stochastic gradient descent algorithms and their applications
  • Mixed-integer linear programming models in resource allocation
