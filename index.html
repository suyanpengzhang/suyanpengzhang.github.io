Hi, I'm Suyanpeng Zhang.

Postdoctoral Scholar · Industrial Engineering & Management Sciences · Northwestern University

I work with Professor Sanjay Mehrotra on healthcare analytics, dynamic programming, and optimization. I received my Ph.D. in Industrial and Systems Engineering from the University of Southern California in 2024, advised by Professor Sze-Chuan Suen. I also hold an M.S. in Industrial and Operations Engineering from the University of Michigan (2019) and a B.S. in Mathematics from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (2017).

Research Interests

What I'm working on

Healthcare analytics, dynamic programming, and optimization methods — and interdisciplinary work that brings together optimization, statistics, probability, and artificial intelligence.

MDPs in Healthcare

Markov decision processes for medical decision making and infectious disease control.

Stochastic Optimization

Approximate stochastic gradient descent algorithms and their applications.

Resource Allocation

Mixed-integer linear programming models for allocating scarce health resources.
